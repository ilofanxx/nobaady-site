Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · 2026-11-07

In one sentence: other users see only what you choose to show. Your email, phone number, username and photo are registered with us, and only authorised staff can open them, for safety and legal reasons.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is nobody. ("we"). Data protection contact / DPO: dpo@nobaady.com. This policy applies to the nobody. app and this website.

3. Legal bases

We process data (a) to perform our contract with you (running the service and Plus); (b) to comply with legal obligations, including UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on Personal Data Protection, UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 34 of 2021 on cybercrimes, the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and other Gulf laws; (c) for our legitimate interests in security, fraud prevention and moderation, balanced against your rights; and (d) with your consent where the law requires it, such as optional analytics. You can withdraw consent at any time in settings.

4. What others see and what we see

  • What other users see. Your username or your generated alias, and your photo only if you turn it on. They never see your email, phone number, account ID, device or IP address.
  • What we hold. Your email, phone number, username, alias and photo are recorded on your account even when hidden, and our servers know which account and device sent each message. Access is limited to authorised staff and every access is logged. This is what allows reporting, blocking, bans and responding to lawful requests.
  • Messages in one-to-one and Trusted chats disappear from both screens when a chat ends. A limited safety log (account IDs, timestamps, and message content needed to investigate reports) is retained for the period in the table below, then deleted.
  • Encryption. Messages are encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. They are not end-to-end encrypted: our authorised staff can read any stored message, including messages in One-to-one chats and Trusted sessions, to review reports, investigate abuse, fraud or risk to someone's safety, enforce our Terms, or when required by law. Each access needs a written reason and is recorded in an audit log that cannot be edited. We do not read messages for advertising or any other purpose.

6. Who we share data with

  • Our moderation team, under access controls and audit logs, to review reports.
  • Service providers that host our servers, run our servers and database (Supabase) and send sign-in codes and safety alerts by email (Resend), under contracts that limit their use of data.
  • Apple and Google, which process payments. We only receive subscription status, not card data.
  • Law enforcement, courts and regulators when we receive a lawful request, when we are required to report (for example child sexual abuse material to NCMEC or the competent Gulf authority), or when necessary to prevent imminent harm.
  • A successor if our company is sold or merged, under the same protections.

7. Where data is stored and cross-border transfers

Our servers are located in Mumbai, India. If you use nobody. from a Gulf country, your data may be transferred to and processed in that region. Where required by the UAE PDPL (Federal Decree-Law 45/2021), the Saudi PDPL or other applicable laws, we rely on an adequacy decision, approved contractual safeguards, or your explicit consent, and we apply the same protections wherever the data is held.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law you may: access the personal data we hold about you; export it in a readable format by emailing dpo@nobaady.com; you can see your account details in Me → My data; correct your username, photo, email, phone number and settings; delete your account and data (Me → Delete account); object to or restrict certain processing; and withdraw consent. You may also complain to the UAE Data Office, the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA) or your local regulator. To exercise a right not available in-app, email dpo@nobaady.com from within the app so we can verify your account; we respond within 30 days.

9. Children

nobody. is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to a minor, we terminate it and delete its data, keeping only what the law requires. If you believe a minor is using nobody., tell us at safety@nobaady.com. See our Child Safety Standards.

10. Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, audit logging of moderator actions, and regular reviews. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data we will notify you and the regulator as the law requires.

11. Changes

We will post updates here and notify you in the app of material changes at least 14 days before they take effect.

Challenges and rewards data

When you take part in challenges we keep: your Nova points and why you earned them, your daily streak, who invited you and whom you invited (you see counts only, never other people's purchases), offer codes delivered to you, your weekly answers (public ones show your display name, private ones are seen only by nobody.), and your likes and votes. If you win a physical prize we may ask for proof of age and identity and a delivery address, used only for delivery and deleted within 90 days after it. Your answers, likes and votes are deleted when you delete your account.
